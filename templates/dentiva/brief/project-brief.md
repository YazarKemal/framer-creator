# Project Brief — Dentiva Dental Clinic Framer Template

---

## Project background

Dentiva is a Framer Marketplace template designed for premium dental clinics.
The dental sector has a wide gap between dated, low-trust clinic websites and
the kind of polished digital presence that patients now expect from any
healthcare provider. Most clinic sites rely on generic templates with stock
photography, aggressive marketing copy, and poor mobile experiences.

Dentiva closes that gap. It gives clinics a refined, editor-friendly website
that feels as professional as their clinical work — without requiring a
custom design engagement. It ships as a Framer template so that clinics (or
the agencies that serve them) can remix, customise, and publish quickly.

The name "Dentiva" is a template brand for the Marketplace listing. Buyers
will replace it with their own clinic name. The placeholder clinic used
throughout the template is fictional and clearly presented as editable
demonstration content.

## Business objectives

1. **Primary:** Generate appointment bookings through the website
2. **Secondary:** Educate prospective patients about treatments so they arrive
   at a consultation informed and ready to proceed
3. **Tertiary:** Build trust through clinician profiles, facility
   transparency, and patient testimonials
4. **Long-term:** Position the clinic as the top-of-mind choice for dental
   care in its region

- Understand what treatments are available and whether they're right for me

## Trust barriers

Dental care carries unusually high trust barriers. Patients are anxious about
pain, cost, competency, and being upsold unnecessary treatments. The template
must address these systematically.

| Barrier | Design response |
|---------|----------------|
| "Will it hurt?" | Transparent treatment process explanations, calm language, patient testimonials that mention comfort |
| "Is this dentist qualified?" | Detailed clinician profiles with credentials, registrations, and continuing education |
| "Am I being overcharged?" | Clear pricing page with ranges, no hidden fees, insurance and payment plan information |
| "Is the clinic clean and modern?" | Photography of the actual facility (or high-quality placeholder), calm and uncluttered design |
| "What if something goes wrong?" | Process transparency, aftercare information, clear contact channels |
| "Is this a chain or a real practice?" | Individual clinician profiles, clinic philosophy, community involvement |
| "Can I trust these reviews?" | Testimonials labelled as demonstration content, verified-badge concept, no exaggerated claims |

## Conversion strategy

### Primary conversion path

1. Visitor lands on a treatment page or the Home page
2. Browses treatment information or clinician profiles
3. Reads testimonials or pricing information
4. Clicks a CTA (primary: "Book an appointment")
5. Fills in the contact/appointment form
6. Receives a confirmation message with next steps

### Secondary conversion path (informational)

1. Visitor reads Journal articles or Treatment Detail pages
2. Subscribes to email updates (optional newsletter sign-up)
3. Returns later when ready to book

### CTA placement rules

- **Above the fold on Home:** Primary CTA in hero
- **After every major section on Home:** Contextual CTA (not the same button repeated — relevant to the section content)
- **Treatment Detail pages:** CTA after the treatment description and after FAQ
- **Sticky mobile CTA:** A fixed button on mobile for appointment booking
- **Footer:** Appointment link in the footer CTA column

## Content strategy

### Content principles

- Write in plain English at approximately a Grade 8 reading level
- Define dental terms on first use (e.g. "endodontics (root canal treatment)")
- Use short paragraphs (2–4 sentences) with clear headings
- Answer the question the patient is actually asking, not the one the clinic
  wants to answer
- Avoid superlatives and unverifiable claims ("best dentist in the region")
- Frame treatment benefits factually: "Many patients find that veneers…" not
  "Veneers will transform your life"

### Content structure per treatment

Every treatment detail page follows the same structure:
1. What this treatment is
2. Who it's for
3. What happens during the procedure
4. Recovery and aftercare
5. How long results last
6. Price range (presented as a guide, not a quote)
7. Frequently asked questions
8. Related treatments

### Photography direction

- **Clinical photography:** Clean, well-lit treatment rooms and equipment —
  convey hygiene and modernity
- **Clinician portraits:** Warm but professional headshots against a neutral
  background — no crossed arms, natural smiles
- **Patient photography:** Avoid the "perfect smile" stock cliché; use
  natural, diverse portraits that feel like real people
- **Facility photography:** Reception area, waiting room, treatment room —
  show the real environment
- **No:** Aggressive before-and-after galleries, visibly anxious patients,
  cartoonish illustrations of teeth

## Accessibility principles

Dentiva is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Key commitments:

- **Colour contrast:** All text meets 4.5:1 minimum (normal text) and 3:1
  (large text) against its background
- **Keyboard navigation:** Every interactive element is reachable and operable
  via keyboard
- **Focus states:** Visible focus rings on all interactive elements — no
  `:focus { outline: none }` without a replacement
- **Form labels:** Every form field has a visible label and programmatic label
- **Alt text:** Every content image has descriptive alt text; decorative
  images are marked as such
- **Heading hierarchy:** Pages use a single `<h1>` with logical heading levels
  below it
- **Motion:** No auto-playing video or animation; scroll-triggered reveals are
  subtle and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44×44px on mobile
- **Link text:** Descriptive link text — no "click here" or "read more"

## Medical-content limitations

This template must not make any clinical claims, promises, or guarantees.
Specifically:

- ❌ "Our implants last a lifetime"
- ❌ "We are the leading clinic for…"
- ❌ "99% success rate"
- ❌ "Pain-free treatment"
- ❌ "Same-day smile transformation"
- ✅ "Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing
  teeth. We'll discuss expected longevity during your consultation."
- ✅ "We use modern techniques and equipment to make your treatment as
  comfortable as possible."

All medical content in the template is **demonstration copy** — clearly
labelled as placeholder content that the buyer must replace with their own
clinically accurate information.

The template does not provide:
- Medical advice
- Treatment recommendations
- Clinical decision-support tools
- Diagnostic questionnaires that suggest a diagnosis

## Scope

### In scope (core template)

- 10 pages as listed in the README
- 4 CMS collections with sample entries
- Appointment booking form with client-side validation
- Full design system implemented as Framer Variables and Styles
- Responsive layouts at all three breakpoints
- SEO metadata on every page
- Open Graph defaults
- Cookie consent notice
- 404 page

### Out of scope (core template)

- Custom React/TypeScript Code Components
- Third-party integrations (Calendly, payment processing, EHR systems)
- Patient portal or login functionality
- Multi-language support (English only in core template)
- Dark mode
- Real-time availability checking or live booking
- HIPAA/GDPR compliance tooling (the template provides placeholder legal
  pages; the buyer is responsible for their own compliance)
- Email marketing automation
- Analytics integration (the buyer adds their own tracking ID)
