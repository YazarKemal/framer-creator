### FAQ

**Eyebrow:**
Common questions

**Section heading:**
You probably have questions —
we have answers

**Accordion items:**

**Do I need to see a dentist if nothing hurts?**
Yes. Many dental problems — including gum disease, cavities, and even oral
cancer — develop without pain in their early stages. Regular check-ups catch
issues when they're smaller, easier to treat, and less expensive. We recommend
a visit every six months for most patients.

**How do I choose between a crown, a bridge, and an implant?**
The right choice depends on the condition of your tooth and the surrounding
teeth, your jawbone health, your budget, and your long-term goals. During
your consultation, we'll explain the pros and cons of each option in the
context of your specific situation — including expected longevity, cost, and
the treatment timeline. There's no single "best" answer; there's the best
answer for you.

**Will my treatment hurt?**
We take comfort seriously. For most procedures, local anaesthetic ensures you
won't feel pain — you may feel pressure or vibration, but not sharp
discomfort. For patients with dental anxiety, we offer sedation options and
take extra time to help you feel at ease. After treatment, any soreness is
usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief and subsides within a
few days.

**How much does a dental implant cost?**
The total cost of an implant includes the consultation, the implant placement,
the abutment, and the crown. It varies depending on whether additional
procedures (such as bone grafting or a sinus lift) are needed. Please see
our pricing page for a guide, and book a consultation for a personalised
quote based on your specific needs.

**Do you treat children?**
Yes. Dr. Yusuf leads our paediatric programme, and all our clinicians are
experienced in treating children. We recommend a first visit by age one, or
within six months of the first tooth appearing — whichever comes first.

**Can I book online?**
Yes. Use the "Book an appointment" button anywhere on the site to fill in our
appointment request form. We'll confirm your appointment by email or phone
within one business day.

**What if I need to cancel or reschedule?**
We understand that plans change. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if
you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may incur a fee. We'll
always work with you to find a new time that suits.

**Do you offer emergency appointments?**
Yes. If you're in pain or have a dental injury, call us as early as possible
and we'll prioritise seeing you — often the same day. For after-hours
emergencies, our voicemail provides contact information for the on-call
clinician.

### Content sections

**What are dental implants?**
A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone to
replace the root of a missing tooth. Once the implant has integrated with the
bone, a custom-made crown is attached to the top. The result looks, feels, and
functions like a natural tooth.

Implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth (using a bridge supported
by implants), or a full arch (using implant-supported dentures).

**Who is this treatment for?**
Implants may be suitable if you have one or more missing teeth, have healthy
gums, and have enough jawbone to support the implant. If bone density is a
concern, bone grafting may be an option — we'll discuss this during your
assessment. Implants are typically recommended for adults whose jaw has
finished growing.

**What happens during the procedure?**
1. **Consultation and planning** — We'll take digital scans and x-rays to
   assess your bone structure and plan the implant position precisely.
2. **Implant placement** — The titanium post is placed into the jawbone
   under local anaesthetic. The procedure typically takes 1–2 hours for a
   single implant.
3. **Healing period** — Over the next 3–6 months, the implant bonds with
   your jawbone (a process called osseointegration). A temporary tooth or
   bridge may be worn during this period.
4. **Crown placement** — Once the implant is stable, we attach the
   custom-designed crown. The colour, shape, and fit are matched to your
   natural teeth.

**Recovery and aftercare**
Most patients return to normal activities within a day or two. Some swelling
and mild discomfort is normal and usually managed with over-the-counter pain
relief. We'll see you for follow-up visits to monitor healing. Once your
implant is complete, it needs the same care as natural teeth: brushing,
flossing, and regular check-ups.

**How long do implants last?**
With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last for many
years — often decades. The crown may need replacement eventually due to normal
wear, but the implant post itself is designed to be a long-term solution.
